## Findings

1. Viewers leave at 8-12 s regardless of length (34 s and 77 s alike). Everything after ~40 s is seen by almost nobody. Target was 25-35 s (27.10), but 8 stakan shipped at 61 s and upcoming scripts are 50-70 s. Biggest issue.
2. Shares are 100-165 on every older reel regardless of reach (769-2360). Looks like one fixed group sharing (friends / Telegram groups), not organic spread. 8 stakan: 1 share after 1 day. Hypothesis, not verifiable. Action: capture that group with an own Telegram channel.
3. Top 3 by views: shortest reel, simple question hook, Javohir on camera.
4. Carousels reach only followers (~10x less than reels). Use them as companions to reels, not standalone.
5. Still not measured: hold@3s and non-follower share (need IG Insights retention screenshots).
